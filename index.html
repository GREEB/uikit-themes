UI Kit themes for Deadlock

Pick a theme, change its colours and font, and install it into your game. Everything happens in this page on your PC: it reads the game files it needs from your Deadlock folder and writes the theme into its addons folder. Nothing is uploaded. Needs Chrome or Edge, and Deadlock closed while installing.

Steam › steamapps › common › Deadlock

Your mods need citadel/addons turned on (a mod manager does that). Themes only restyle; they don't need the UI Kit runtime. To remove a theme, delete its pakNN_dir.vpk from the addons folder, or install another one in its place.
